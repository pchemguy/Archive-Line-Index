# Plan and layout review

For read-only review, inspect the relevant PLAN root and children, layout, accepted design and SPEC, active feature plan if any, and the current repository tree where placement matters. Check that phase and milestone exits are objective, dependencies and integration points are feasible, every significant API/model/contract owner has a delivery and verification route, and physical ownership supports the logical boundaries. Check refinement prerequisites before dependent consumer work without inventing behavior in PLAN/layout. Report concrete gaps and conflicting authorities without editing governing documents.

Assess every phase against the [delivery scope procedure](delivery-plan.md#phase-scope-and-version-coverage) before considering its counts. Establish its integrated functionality outcome, included/excluded scope, predecessor baseline, dependencies and applicable exits. Could independent scope move to a subsequent coherent increment without undermining this outcome? Require a consequential rationale for retained scope and necessary enabling/risk stages. Reject an oversized later phase or final backlog bucket as well as an oversized first phase; disconnected technical layers do not establish functional increments.

For greenfield Phase 1, additionally check the simplest sensible usable core. An early small milestone within a complete-version phase is insufficient, as is a running skeleton missing essential core use. For existing systems/features, assess the bounded changed outcome without imposing a new MVP. Review full-version coverage across phases separately from each phase's actual acceptance. Version scope and document preapproval do not establish a single-phase override; any actual explicit shape exception and its consequences remain visible.

Check milestones are meaningful bounded steps within their phase, with inclusions/deferrals, justified prerequisites and timely integration/regression evidence. Preserve prior working behavior at every phase exit rather than deferring integration to the end. At suitable milestones, check demonstrable functionality, relevant usability/risk evidence and the human continue/amend/simplify/stop decision; technical readiness and product usefulness each need support.

Check that strategy belongs in PLAN, physical ownership in layout, behavior in SPEC, and executable units and status in TASKS or active FEATURE-TASKS. Check alignment with PROJECT, ARCHITECTURE, DECOMPOSITION, and SPEC. Review parent-child links and component-to-path routing in both directions, including tests, documentation, and integration. Read the main PLAN and layout nodes as standalone end-state descriptions without editing-history language; genuine compatibility and transition obligations may refer to an earlier released state.

Report concrete gaps and affected task-list dependencies. Direct corrections belong to [delivery plan](delivery-plan.md) or [physical layout](physical-layout.md); incorporation of accepted feature documents belongs to **sdd-integrate-feature**. A review does not authorize edits, task execution, or progression into another workflow.

For multipass, compare against prior-pass PLAN/TASKS under the [delivery mode](../../sdd-manage/references/doc-design-modes.md#multipass-delivery-modes). Strict requires unchanged earlier phase/task identities, scope, membership, order, dependencies and exits. Flexible amendments require explicit permission, a small change fitting the earlier scope and retained range/rationale; recheck affected contracts and exits. Checkpoint preapproval or autoloop is not flexible permission. Report conflicts rather than silently restructuring protected ranges.

## Required PLAN/SPEC QC gate

Apply **sdd-conventions**' **Development-document QC**. Establish current SPEC/design QC evidence for the accepted inputs; a material unresolved specification issue blocks dependent planning. Map every significant accepted contract and end-to-end obligation to a planned delivery route, including honest deferrals, failures, dependencies and objective exits. Identify omissions, duplication, invented features and scope drift. Verify that layout supports the accepted structure and planned integration; a layout-only review reports its affected PLAN implications without authorizing PLAN edits.

For each phase, report delivery milestone count separately from its final dedicated phase review milestone. Apply shared 3–5 guidance, explicit 1–2 fragmentation and 10+ overload/drift assessments, departure rationale and empty-group rules. Inspect semantic scope even for in-range groups: contract/component breadth, delayed usefulness, dependency chains, integration/testing effort and boundary overhead. Keep justified narrow phases; add no padding or quota-driven splits. Milestone review outcomes remain required; standalone review tasks follow the [lifecycle threshold](../../sdd-conventions/references/backend-object-lifecycle.md#explicit-review-units), with omitted tasks' obligations assigned to the final implementation task. The special phase review milestone is unchanged and excluded from implementation-milestone counts.

Authoring includes authorized bounded PLAN/layout correction, recheck and `PLAN-REVIEW-REPORT.md` adjacent to PLAN (FEATURE-PLAN counterpart beside its root); the report covers applicable children and relevant layout. Use sdd-report's **Document QC reports** format and append Revision N cycles. Route changed behavioral/design decisions upstream; never rewrite SPEC to fit PLAN. Persist the corrected strategy and review together before TASKS generation. Pure review does not amend strategy. Missing/stale evidence or confirmed unresolved issues blocks dependent generation; explicit exceptions remain recorded as exceptions, not Ready.

## Human checkpoints

- **PLAN:** Present the completed and published PLAN for human review before preparing a dependent layout.
- **layout:** Present the completed and published layout for human review before generating TASKS.

Apply [human document review](../../sdd-manage/references/human-document-review.md) for acceptance and applicable preapproval.
