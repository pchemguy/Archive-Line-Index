# Argument review

## Scope and authority

Examine claims, premises, assumptions, inference, counterarguments, qualifications, contradictions, fallacies, and blind spots. Use for critical review or development where reasoning matters. Diagnose and propose repairs without silently changing the author's position. Explain the defect; a fallacy label alone is not useful. Empirical verification belongs to evidence work.

## Inputs and missing information

Use the text, purpose, audience, and available support. Identify excerpt limits. Reconstruct implicit reasoning charitably, labeling the reconstruction as inference rather than inserting it into the author's argument. If the conclusion or intended position is unclear, ask before proposing a substantive replacement.

## Procedure

1. Identify central and subordinate claims, supporting reasons, and the conclusions they are meant to sustain. Keep observations, explanations, predictions, value judgments, and recommendations distinct.
2. Reconstruct the reasoning chain: premises → inference → conclusion. Identify assumptions, definitions, scope, comparators, and omitted connecting steps.
3. Test internal consistency and the appropriate inference standard. For a claimed necessary conclusion, check deductive validity: does it follow from the premises, or require an unstated premise? For a probabilistic or provisional conclusion, assess support strength, plausible alternatives, and calibrated certainty rather than require entailment. Are terms or scope shifting? Does the reasoning confuse correlation with causation, possibility with probability, or absence of evidence with evidence of absence?
4. Test proportionality. Is certainty stronger than the support? Does a local example justify a broad conclusion? Are numerical comparisons using compatible quantities, units, denominators, and populations?
5. Consider alternative explanations and the strongest relevant counterarguments. Do not manufacture objections for balance or treat unequal evidence as equivalent. Identify what observation would discriminate between explanations when useful.
6. Examine framing and blind spots: omitted stakeholders, trade-offs, boundary conditions, counterexamples, selection effects, and unjustified assumptions. Tie each concern to the actual argument rather than a generic checklist.
7. Separate logical problems from support needs. Use [evidence review](evidence-review.md) when a premise requires factual assessment; resolve [evidence mode](manager.md#evidence-modes-and-selection) before independent research.
8. Recommend repairs: clarify a premise, narrow scope, qualify certainty, supply support, address a counterargument, distinguish a value choice, or reconsider the conclusion. State which repairs preserve meaning and which need an author decision.

## Outputs

Return prioritized findings with claim/location, reconstructed reasoning where needed, defect, consequence, and repair. For substantial arguments, a claim–premise–assumption register may help; avoid it for a single sentence. A request for critique produces critique, not a rewritten advocacy piece.

## Examples and failure handling

- "Error rates fell after training; training caused the decrease" requires ruling out relevant alternatives or qualifying causation. Do not independently assert an alternative cause without evidence.
- "Ten users preferred A, so all users will prefer A" has a generalization and certainty problem. Recommend a population-limited statement and identify sampling questions.
- "The nominal mass equals explosive yield" may confuse quantities. Identify the incompatible comparison and route the factors and source claims to evidence review; preserve essential calculations in any shorter revision.
- If a premise is merely unfamiliar to the model, label a verification need rather than pronounce it false. If the supplied support defeats the proposed conclusion, explain that strengthening the prose cannot repair the reasoning.

## Completion checks

The review addresses the actual argument, labels reconstructions, distinguishes reasoning from empirical verification, and proposes actionable repairs. No fabricated counterargument, false balance, or silent change to position, certainty, scope, or causality appears.
